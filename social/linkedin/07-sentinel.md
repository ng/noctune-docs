## Post

```text
Most veterinary teams can name the client who made the room go quiet.
Very few have a record of what was actually said.

Hostile client interactions are a real part of this job, and they're hard to document after the fact.

noctune Sentinel is a documentation aid for those moments:

→ It reviews appointments you already chose to record, and never turns on a microphone itself
→ It flags confrontational language or explicit refusal of recommended care
→ Timestamped markers jump to the exact moment in the transcript and audio
→ The audio for a flagged visit is kept beyond the standard retention window

A flag is a reason to review the recording, not a verdict. Sentinel doesn't contact anyone. Your team's own policies decide what happens next.

A careful question for this community: when a client interaction goes badly, how does your clinic document it today, if at all?

#VeterinaryMedicine #VetMed #CompassionFatigue
```
