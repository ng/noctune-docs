## Post

```text
The appointment went perfectly.
Then the Wi-Fi dropped, and the recording didn't make it.

If you've ever lost a recording, you know it makes you trust any tool less.

This video covers the unglamorous part of a scribe: getting the audio in, safely.

→ Record in the browser or on iPhone
→ Pause and resume when you step out of the room
→ Upload recordings you already have
→ Recover an interrupted recording and retry an unfinished upload

Nothing gets drafted until the audio arrives, so this is the part we work hardest to get right.

Question for anyone who has recorded appointments: where does it go wrong in your clinic? Connectivity in exam rooms, phones dying, forgetting to press record, or something else?

#VeterinaryMedicine #VetMed #VetTech
```
