# 6. Keep the practice organized

Video: planned (`practice-organized`)

## Post

```text
"Has anyone seen this patient before?"
Somewhere, the answer is in a chart nobody has time to dig through.

Documentation only helps if the next person can find it. This video covers the parts of noctune that hold a practice together:

→ Patient records with every encounter in one place
→ Past visits beside the current note, searchable by terms like a medication or weight
→ Search patients, encounters, and messages from the top bar, down to a drug or symptom
→ An encounter board that opens on today and shows what still needs signing
→ A practice workspace with roles for vets, techs, and front desk

A single appointment is a note. A year of them is a patient's history.

Practice managers and owners: what's the hardest thing to keep consistent when several doctors see the same patients?

#VeterinaryMedicine #VetMed #PracticeManagement
```
