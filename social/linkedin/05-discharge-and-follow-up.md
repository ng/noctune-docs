## Post

```text
The client nodded through the whole discharge.
Two days later: "What dose was that again?"

Discharge instructions get forgotten, and follow-up questions come back to wherever they can reach you. For many vets, that's their personal email.

This video covers what happens after the note:

→ Review the discharge summary before anything is sent
→ Choose the recipient, and add an email template if you need one
→ Pick where replies go: noctune Nest, no-reply, or your personal email
→ With Nest, the client replies to a private relay address, your personal email stays hidden, and the reply comes back linked to that visit

Every account can send discharge notes. Nest adds the private, two-way part.

How do clients reach you with follow-up questions today: the front desk, the clinic inbox, your own email, or texts you'd rather not get?

```
