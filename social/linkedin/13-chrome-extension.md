# 13. Browser extension

Draft only; not scheduled or published.

  `walkthrough-cover.png` as the custom thumbnail (the badge after the first keystroke)

The footage is the real extension panel and shortcut, filmed with fictional patients. The

is shown docked beside it.

## Post

```text
The note is finished in noctune.
Now it has to get into the practice system, one box at a time.

Copy, switch tabs, find the field, paste. Five times per patient.

The noctune browser extension puts your note in a panel beside the patient record:

→ Click a field and press one key: the next section goes in
→ Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan, then the discharge instructions
→ Works with any practice system that runs in a browser. No integration needed

It never saves or sends anything for you. You read the record and press Save yourself.

How many times a day does your team copy and paste between noctune and your practice system?

#VeterinaryMedicine #VetMed #VetTech
```
