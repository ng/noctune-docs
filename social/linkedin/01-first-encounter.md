Most vets I talk to describe the same thing: the appointment ends, and the documentation starts.

This is a 90-second walk through one visit in noctune, from recording to client follow-up:

→ Record the appointment in the browser, or upload audio you already have
→ Get a draft SOAP note and discharge summary from general templates
→ Click a citation in the note to jump to that moment in the transcript and audio
→ Edit, save, and mark it complete when your review is done
→ Send discharge instructions, and keep client replies tied to the visit

The draft is a starting point. The clinical judgment stays yours, which is why every claim in the note points back to the recording.

The patient in the video is fictional. The problem isn't.
