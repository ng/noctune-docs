## Post

```text
It's 8:40 p.m. The last patient left three hours ago.
You're still writing the note for the second appointment of the day.

Most vets I talk to describe the same thing: the appointment ends, and the documentation starts.

This is a 90-second walk through one visit in noctune, from recording to client follow-up:

→ Record the appointment in the browser, or upload audio you already have
→ Get a draft SOAP note and discharge summary from general templates
→ Click a citation in the note to jump to that moment in the transcript and audio
→ Edit, save, and mark it complete when your review is done
→ Send discharge instructions, and keep client replies tied to the visit

The draft is a starting point. The clinical judgment stays yours, which is why citations in the note point back to the recording.

The patient in the video is fictional. The problem isn't.

For the vets and techs here: when do you actually finish your notes? Between appointments, end of day, or at home after dinner?

#VeterinaryMedicine #VetMed #Veterinarian
```

Record in the exam room. Finish the note at your desk.

This video follows one fictional appointment:

→ Start on iPhone: pick the patient and the general SOAP and discharge templates
→ Record, pause, and resume, then let it upload in the background
→ Open the same appointment on the web when the draft is ready
→ Check a citation against the audio and transcript, make an edit, save, and complete
→ Send discharge instructions, with client replies kept alongside the visit

Every account can send discharge notes. Replies through a private relay address are part of noctune Nest.
