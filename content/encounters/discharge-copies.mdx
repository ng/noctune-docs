# Copy your discharges to a clinic inbox

Many practices keep a copy of every discharge in a shared inbox so it can be filed in the PIMS. noctune can add that inbox as a hidden copy (Bcc) on each discharge you send to a client, so you don't have to type it every time.

You can copy discharges in two ways:

- **Bcc one send.** Add an address under **Bcc** in the send window. It gets that discharge only.
- **Always Bcc.** Save an address once. After the address is confirmed, it gets a hidden copy of every discharge you send, from the website or the iPhone app.

To get the discharge _draft_ by email when a note is ready, before anything goes to the client, see [Email me the discharge too](/settings/account#email-me-the-discharge-too).

## Always Bcc a clinic inbox

1. On a ready encounter, open the **Discharge Notes** tab, choose **Send Email**, then **Send discharge notes**. The **Send Discharge Notes** window opens.
2. Add the client under **To**. Choose **Bcc** at the right end of the **To** field, then type the clinic inbox address in the **Bcc** field.
3. Tick **Always Bcc this address**. The window explains what happens next: "We’ll send _address_ a confirmation link. Once confirmed, it gets a hidden copy of every discharge you send, from web or iPhone. Change this in Settings → Account."
4. Choose **Send + Complete**, or **Send only** from the dropdown. After the encounter is completed, the button is **Send**.
5. This discharge goes to the Bcc address straight away. noctune then saves the address and emails it a confirmation link, and the message after sending reads "Sent. Check _address_ for the confirmation link." Hidden copies of later discharges start once someone with access to that inbox [confirms the address](#for-the-person-receiving-copies).
6. To change or remove the address, go to **Settings → Account** and find **Copy every discharge I send**. See [Discharge copy addresses](/settings/account#copy-every-discharge-i-send).

**Always Bcc this address** appears only when the **Bcc** field holds exactly one valid address that isn't already saved. If noctune can't save the address, the discharge is still sent, and the message after sending gives the reason. For example, the list may already be full.

You can also add addresses directly in [Account settings](/settings/account#copy-every-discharge-i-send) without sending a discharge.

## What the send window shows

Saved addresses aren't added to the **Bcc** field. noctune adds them when it sends. One line under **To** shows where saved copies go:

- **Waiting for _address_ to confirm.** The address hasn't confirmed yet and gets no automatic copies. Choose **Resend link** to email a new confirmation link.
- **A hidden copy also goes to _address_.** The address is confirmed and will get this discharge. Choose **Manage** to open Account settings.

The client never sees the hidden copies. An address that's already in **To** or **Bcc** isn't sent a second copy.

Saved copies also apply to discharges you send from the iPhone app, because noctune adds them when it sends the email.

## For the person receiving copies

This section is for whoever looks after the inbox that receives copies, such as a practice manager or IT.

### The confirmation email

When a veterinarian adds your address, you get an email from noctune titled **Confirm this address for discharge copies**, or **Confirm this address for discharge drafts** for [discharge drafts](/settings/account#email-me-the-discharge-too). It says who is asking, that copies include pet and client details, and that the link works for 7 days. Choose **Review and confirm** to open the confirmation page. You don't need a noctune account.

If you don't recognize the request, ignore the email. noctune sends nothing to an address until someone confirms it.

### The confirmation page

The page asks **Send discharge copies to this address?** (or **Send discharge drafts to this address?**). It names the veterinarian, and their clinic when they have one, and shows the address. It also shows how long the link has left.

- Opening the page changes nothing, so a mail security scanner that follows the link can't confirm it. Only the **Confirm** button does.
- After you choose **Confirm**, the page reads **You’re set**. Copies start with the next discharge.
- **Let us know** on the page opens an email to noctune support if you weren't expecting the request.

Confirming for discharge copies doesn't sign the address up for discharge drafts, and confirming for drafts doesn't sign it up for copies. Each needs its own confirmation.

### If the link no longer works

The page reads **This link no longer works** when:

- the link is more than 7 days old,
- the veterinarian sent a newer link, which replaces the old one, or
- the address was removed.

Nothing has been sent to the address. Ask the veterinarian to send a new confirmation from noctune. Opening the link again after you've confirmed shows **You’re set**.

### What copies look like

- **Discharge copies** are hidden copies of the discharge email the client receives, sent at the same time.
- **Discharge drafts** arrive when a note is ready, before anything goes to the client. Each one is titled **Discharge draft** with the pet’s name, says who shared it, and says it hasn't been sent to the client.

### Stopping copies

Copies have no unsubscribe link. To stop them, ask the veterinarian to remove your address in noctune. Once it's removed, nothing more is sent to it.

## Limits and privacy

- Copies and drafts include pet and client details. Add only inboxes your practice controls.
- You can save up to 5 addresses for discharge copies and up to 5 for discharge drafts.
- Each send can go to at most 50 addresses across **To** and **Bcc**, including saved copies. If saved copies would go over that limit, the send is blocked with an error.
- A **Bcc** address typed for one send gets that discharge right away, without confirmation. Only saved addresses need to be confirmed.
- Deleting your noctune account removes your saved addresses.
