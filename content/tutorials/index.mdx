# Watch noctune in short walkthroughs

Short, narrated walkthroughs of noctune on the web and iPhone. Each one uses fictional demo patients and records real app footage. Captions are on by default; use the player's captions button to turn them off.

## Pick a topic

- [Getting started](/tutorials/getting-started) — Your first day: choose how notes and client discharges come out, open your first encounter, check your Account settings, invite your team, and see what to set up next.
- [Your first encounter](/tutorials/first-encounter) — One fictional appointment from start to finish: record or upload audio, review the SOAP draft and discharge summary, check citations against the recording, complete the note, and send follow-up.
- [Record and recover](/tutorials/record-and-recover) — Record in the browser or on iPhone, upload audio you already have, and pick up a recording that was interrupted or never finished uploading.
- [Review with confidence](/tutorials/review-with-confidence) — Search the transcript, jump from a citation to the moment it was said, correct the note, and mark it complete when your review is done.
- [Make templates your own](/tutorials/templates) — Browse included and community templates, duplicate one to adapt, start your own, or import a template you already use and let noctune organize it.
- [Discharge and follow-up](/tutorials/discharge-follow-up) — Review what the client will receive, choose how replies are routed, and find client answers on the encounter instead of in your external email inbox.
- [Messages](/tutorials/messages) — Sort client replies with Unread and Flagged, open the visit a reply belongs to, answer from the encounter with a template, then assign or archive the thread.
- [Email templates](/tutorials/email-templates) — Edit a reusable client email, add a placeholder, and insert it from an encounter so the patient's name and practice details fill in before you send.
- [Sentinel](/tutorials/sentinel) — See how a flagged encounter looks, hear the marked moment in the original audio, and find related client messages before you reply.
- [Patients and history](/tutorials/patients-history) — Find a patient by pet or owner name, read every visit and its status, compare today's note with an earlier one, and archive patients you no longer see.
