# Work through client replies, one visit at a time

Sort the inbox with Unread and Flagged, open the encounter a reply belongs to, answer from that encounter with an email template, assign the thread to a teammate, and archive it when it is handled.

## On iPhone

The iPhone Messages tab lists **Inbound**, **Sent**, and **All** mail, labels Sentinel-flagged threads, and starts a **New Message**. Replies linked to a visit also appear on that encounter's Messages tab; [Discharge and follow-up](/tutorials/discharge-follow-up) shows it. The **Unread** and **Flagged** filters, assignment, and **Archive** are web only, so this topic has no iPhone video.
