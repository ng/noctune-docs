# Dental charts from a dictated exam

> **Early preview.** Dental charting covers dogs and cats, and every chart is a draft until you
> review and confirm it. The charts in this video come from invented sample dictations, read by
> synthetic voices. The dictation you hear is that sample recording, played by the app.

Choose the Dental Procedure template and dictate the exam as you do now. noctune writes the note,
then a chart of the mouth, tooth by tooth. Open a flagged tooth and play back what was said,
compare with the patient's last chart, then confirm it and download or email it.
