# Keep the parts of the note you've already reviewed

Edit one section of the SOAP note and it locks, so new recordings and regenerations can't change it. Regenerate a section that's still unlocked, review the proposed rewrite, then apply or discard it. Practice templates are locked too: owners and admins set the standard, and members use or duplicate it.
