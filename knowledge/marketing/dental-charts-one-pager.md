# noctune dental charts: an early preview

**Early preview.** This feature is new and still changing. Every chart shown here was built from
an invented sample dictation, read aloud by a synthetic voice, about a fictional dog or cat. No
real patients or clinicians are involved.

## What it is

You dictate a dental exam the way you already do. noctune writes the note, and next to it a chart
of the dog's or cat's mouth: each tooth you mentioned, by Triadan number, drawn on the mouth and
written out in the quadrant lists beside it, with a short summary. Where noctune is unsure (a
misheard tooth number, a side that may be yours rather than the patient's, a score off its scale)
it flags the tooth instead of guessing. Open any tooth to play back the moment it was dictated, and
mark it reviewed once you've checked it. You can compare with the patient's earlier chart, then
confirm it and download, copy or email it. On a dog or cat encounter without a chart, **See a
sample chart** shows a finished chart of a fictional dog or cat before you dictate your own.
**A chart is a draft until you review and confirm it.**

## What it covers today

- Dogs and cats, adult teeth. Baby teeth go in a tooth's note.
- Gingivitis and calculus indices, mobility, furcation, periodontal stage, the deepest probing
  depth and where it was probed, findings (and where on the tooth) and treatments with AVDC codes,
  the procedure (extraction or crown amputation), and a free-text note per tooth.
- Teeth with a finding on part of the tooth drawn from the side, with the finding marked.
- A PDF or image download, a codes-only text copy for pasting into a practice system, and a
  plain-language copy for the client's discharge email.

## What we'd like your feedback on

1. **The tooth drawings.** Do the dog and cat mouths look right to you: tooth shapes, positions,
   and which teeth are present?
2. **The code list.** Are the AVDC findings and treatments you use offered, and named the way you
   would write them?
3. **The layout.** Does the mouth in the middle with the quadrant lists either side work for you,
   on screen and on the PDF?
4. **What a chart should show.** What do you expect on a dental chart that isn't here, and what
   would you leave off?

Reply with anything, however brief. Screenshots of your own charts with patient details removed
are very welcome.
