## Post

```text
9 pm. A client reply lands in your personal inbox.
The chart it belongs to is back at the clinic.

Client replies belong with the visit, but they usually end up wherever the client could reach you. This video shows the Messages inbox in noctune as a workflow:

→ Filter to Unread for new replies, or Flagged for visits Sentinel marked for review
→ Open a reply and jump straight to the encounter it belongs to, with the note beside the conversation
→ Answer from that encounter, starting from a saved email template
→ Assign the thread to a teammate so everyone knows who's answering
→ Archive it when it's handled

Assignment and archiving are on the web for now. The iPhone app shows the inbox, but not those triage tools.

Who answers client follow-up emails at your practice: the doctor who saw the patient, or whoever is free?

#VeterinaryMedicine #VetMed #ClientCommunication
```
