# 15. Dental charts (early preview)

Draft only; not scheduled or published. Rendered locally (about 2 minutes); it still needs a
person's full watch and listen before it is published (`review` in `tutorials/episodes.json`).

Video (from `tutorials/episodes.json`):

- Web: `dental-charts-web` → `walkthrough-captioned.mp4`, with `walkthrough-cover.png` as the
  custom thumbnail (Murphy's chart, set by the story's `cover` time; recheck it after narration)

Every chart in the film comes from an invented sample dictation, read by a synthetic voice, about
fictional dogs and cats. The dictation you hear is that recording, played by the app. The
opening card, the footer, and the closing card all say **early preview**.

The dental is done. The dictation is done.

We're testing dental charting in noctune, as an early preview. Dictate the exam the way you already do, and next to the note noctune draws a chart of the dog's or cat's mouth:

It's early. It covers dogs and cats only, and every chart needs your review. The charts in the video come from sample dictations with synthetic voices.
