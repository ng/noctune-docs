# Manage your account, billing, and notifications

Settings has three tabs for personal identity, billing access, and notification defaults. Team membership and invitations live under [Practice](../practice.md).

## Account

Manage the display name shown in messages, the personal noctune inbox handle, and the **Auto-finish on copy or send** encounter preference.

![Account settings with display-name preview, inbound noctune email, and encounter auto-finish preference](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/settings/account.webp)

See [Account](account.md).

## Billing & Notes

Review the current plan, included capabilities, notes used this month, remaining allowance, add-ons, available packages, and recent activity.

![Billing and Notes settings with Pro plan, included features, usage, add-ons, and recent note activity](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/settings/billing.webp)

See [Billing & Notes](billing.md).

## Notifications

Review the current email and push defaults for encounters, Messages, billing, and product updates, plus the displayed quiet-hours window.

![Notifications settings matrix with Email and Push columns for encounter, message, billing, and product events](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/settings/notifications.webp)

See [Notifications](notifications.md).
