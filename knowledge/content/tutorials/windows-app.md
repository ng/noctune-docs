# Move a note into a Windows practice system

Open the noctune panel beside the patient record in your practice information management system, on the PC or over Remote Desktop or Citrix. Click a field and press Ctrl+Shift+E: the next section of the note goes in. Discharge instructions go in the same way. The app only pastes; it never saves or sends anything in your practice system, so you review the record and save it yourself.
