# Write the client emails you send most, once

Open a handout from the Handouts tab of Templates, add guidance and a placeholder, preview it with a sample patient, then insert it into a message from an encounter and watch the placeholders fill in before you send. Handouts used to be called Email Templates.

## On the web

Tutorial video (email-templates-web) is not yet published.

## On iPhone

Handouts are created, edited, and inserted on the web. The iPhone app does not include the Handouts tab or a handout picker in its composers.
