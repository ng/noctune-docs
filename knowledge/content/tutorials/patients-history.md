# See a patient's whole history before you decide

Find a patient by pet or owner name, read their visits and statuses on one page, compare today's note with an earlier visit, jump to anyone with Command-K, and archive patients you no longer see.

## On the web, with iPhone history

Tutorial video (patients-history-web) is not yet published.

## On iPhone

The Patients tab searches by patient name and shows each patient's visits, newest first. Global search, owner-name search, Past Encounters beside the note, and removing a patient are on the web. See [Patients](../patients.md) and [Navigation & Search](../navigation-and-search.md) for the full guides.
