# Start from a template another clinician shared

Community Templates is a gallery of SOAP and discharge templates that clinicians have chosen to share. It gives you a reviewed starting point without changing the publisher's original.

![Community Templates gallery with Discover, noctune templates, and Shared by me tabs; SOAP and Discharge filters; search; sorting; and three published examples](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/community/browse.webp)

## Browse the gallery

Use the source tabs to switch among **Discover**, **noctune templates**, and **Shared by me**. Filter by **SOAP** or **Discharge**, search the visible gallery, and sort by **Most used**, **Newest**, or **Most liked**.

Each row shows the template purpose, author, description, update time, likes, duplicates, and peer-note count. Choose a row for its full content and discussion.

## Inspect a template

The detail page shows the publisher's description and notes, engagement counts, rendered **Preview**, and source **Markdown**. A like records that the template was useful to you; choose the heart again to remove it.

![Low-Stress Wellness Exam community detail with author, likes, duplicate count, notes count, Preview and Markdown tabs, and Add to action](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/community/detail.webp)

## Duplicate and customize

Choose **Add to… → My templates** to create an editable personal copy. Owners and admins can also add a copy to their active practice. Edits, defaults, and later deletion affect the copy, not the publisher’s original.

## Learn from peer notes

The **Questions** tab supports clinician questions, author replies, helpful votes, and a featured note. Use it to understand the intended workflow or a useful variation before duplicating the template.

![Notes from other vets with a featured question, author reply, helpful counts, and a new-note composer](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/community/notes.webp)

## Report unsafe or inappropriate content

Choose **Report this template** on the detail page. Select the closest reason—Inappropriate, Spam, Plagiarism, Prompt injection, or Other—and add optional context. Filing the dialog sends the report for review; it does not immediately remove the template.

![Report this template dialog with five reasons, optional notes, and File report action](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/community/report.webp)

## Publish your own template

In the [template editor](templates.md), select a saved custom template and choose **More actions → Publish to community**. Review the privacy warning, remove any patient or client information, and optionally add usage notes for other clinicians.

![Publish to community gallery dialog with privacy guidance and notes for other veterinarians](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/templates/publish.webp)

You can unpublish later. Existing duplicates remain in the other clinicians' accounts.
