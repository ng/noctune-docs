# Create your account and sign in for the first time

Use noctune in a browser to record an appointment, upload existing audio, review the transcript, and finish the clinical note. The iOS app can also capture audio and send the encounter to the same workspace.

[Download noctune on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6744552826)

## 1. Sign up or sign in

Open the sign-in page. You can continue with Google or Apple, or enter your work email and password and select **Continue**.

![noctune sign-in page with Google, Apple, and work email sign-in options](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/getting-started/sign-in.webp)

If a practice invited you, sign in with the address that received the invitation.

## 2. Accept the terms

The first time you sign in, noctune shows a **Before you record** step. Review how your recordings are used, the Terms of Service, and the Privacy Policy. **Agree & continue** becomes available after you select all three acknowledgements. noctune asks again only when your account has not accepted the current versions.

![noctune legal acceptance page with the recording, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy acknowledgements](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/getting-started/accept-terms.webp)

On iOS, one more step follows the terms. A one-time **How your recordings are used** screen explains that a submitted recording is sent to Deepgram to turn speech into text and to Amazon Web Services (AWS) to draft your note. Choose **Agree & Continue** to reach the app; on the web, that recording and AI-drafting consent is folded into the **Before you record** step above rather than a separate screen. See [Data Storage & Retention](reference/data-storage.md) for the full path your recording takes.

## 3. Land on the dashboard

After acceptance, noctune opens the Dashboard. It brings the selected day's encounters, recent client replies, outstanding work, activity calendar, and recording shortcut into one view.

![noctune dashboard with encounters, recent messages, work summary, calendar, and start encounter shortcut](https://docs.noctune.ai/screenshots/dashboard/overview.webp)
